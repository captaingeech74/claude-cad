CAD Colour Vision Screener v2.7
================================
Developed by the Applied Vision Research Centre (AVOT)
City St George's, University of London

HOW TO RUN:
1. Double-click "START-TEST.bat" or "cad-screener.exe"
2. Follow the on-screen instructions
3. The screening takes approximately 3 minutes

DISPLAY REQUIREMENTS:
- Set your monitor to sRGB colour mode if available
- Disable any blue-light filters (Night Shift, f.lux, etc.)
- Ensure your room is well-lit without glare on the screen

NOTE: This is a screening tool, not a clinical diagnosis.
For comprehensive colour vision assessment, visit:
https://researchcentres.citystgeorges.ac.uk/applied-vision/avot

For more information, visit our website.
